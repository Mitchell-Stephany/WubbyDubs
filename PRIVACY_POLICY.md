# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** August 2, 2026

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how the Price Arbitrage Tracker Discord bot ("the Bot") collects, uses, and protects your information. By using the Bot, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1. Discord-Provided Information
When you add the Bot to your Discord server, we receive:
- **Server ID**: Unique identifier for your Discord server
- **Channel IDs**: Identifiers for channels where the Bot has permissions
- **User IDs**: Identifiers for users who interact with the Bot (for command processing)

### 2.2. Usage Data
- **Command Usage**: Which commands are used and how frequently
- **Error Logs**: Technical information to diagnose and fix issues
- **Performance Metrics**: Response times and system performance data

### 2.3. Information We Do NOT Collect
- Personal messages or conversations
- Private information beyond Discord-provided IDs
- Financial information or payment details
- IP addresses or location data

## 3. How We Use Your Information

### 3.1. Service Provision
- Send notifications to specified channels
- Process user commands and requests
- Maintain Bot functionality and performance

### 3.2. Service Improvement
- Analyze usage patterns to improve features
- Debug and fix technical issues
- Optimize Bot performance and reliability

### 3.3. Security
- Detect and prevent abuse or unauthorized access
- Comply with Discord's Terms of Service
- Ensure system integrity and security

## 4. Data Storage and Security

### 4.1. Storage Duration
- Discord IDs are stored only while the Bot is in your server
- Usage logs are retained for 30 days for debugging purposes
- Error logs are retained for 7 days

### 4.2. Security Measures
- All data is stored securely with appropriate access controls
- Regular security reviews and updates
- Limited access to user data (only essential personnel)

### 4.3. Data Deletion
- You can request data deletion by removing the Bot from your server
- Alternatively, contact us directly for data removal requests
- Data is permanently deleted within 7 days of request

## 5. Third-Party Services

### 5.1. Discord
- The Bot operates within Discord's infrastructure
- Discord's privacy policy applies to data shared with their platform
- We comply with Discord's data protection requirements

### 5.2. External APIs
- The Bot may use external APIs for price data (retailers, eBay)
- These services have their own privacy policies
- We do not share your Discord information with these services

## 6. Your Rights and Choices

### 6.1. Access and Control
- **Remove Bot**: Remove the Bot from your server to stop data collection
- **Review Data**: Request a copy of data we have about your server
- **Delete Data**: Request deletion of your server's data

### 6.2. Opt-Out
- You can opt out of data collection by removing the Bot
- Some features may not work without certain data collection
